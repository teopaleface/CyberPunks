# Daybreak launch film picture plates

Generated with the built-in image_gen tool. The tool did not report its underlying model name. Every call requested the largest available landscape 16:9 output. Actual selected raw images are 1672x941 pixels; the tool has no explicit resolution selector in this environment.

All pictures depict a synthetic handmade miniature village, not a real site or event. No Git commands were used. Outputs and processing scripts are confined to this directory. The requested macOS upscaler was downloaded into /tmp; the built-in image tool also retains default source copies.

## References and generation

Every attempt received these four visual references, in this order:

1. ../../frames-codex/v7/concepts/planet_storm.png
2. ../../frames-codex/v7/concepts/planet_routes.png
3. ../../frames-codex/v7/concepts/popup_map.png
4. ../../frames-codex/v7/concepts/planet_dawn.png

Attempt 3 for p04_wide and p06_unfold also received the prior selected image as image 5, for a targeted edit. Twenty-one built-in image calls total. Rejected variants remain in attempts/. Final selected originals are in raw/.

## Enlargement and licence

Every final plate was processed with Real-ESRGAN ncnn Vulkan, realesrgan-x4plus, 4x: 1672x941 to 6688x3764. Those candidates are retained as qa/*_esrgan4x.png. Candidates were cover-fitted to 3840x2160 and compared against Lanczos crops in qa/upscale_comparison_*.jpg.

For ALL TEN plates the selected final is the fallback: high-quality Lanczos 2x to 3344x1882, then Lanczos cover-fit to 3840x2160. Real-ESRGAN made the small vehicles, workers, plaster, gravel, foliage or fabric look unnaturally clean and smooth. Lanczos retains the input's photographic texture. This means the deliverables are 4K files, not native 4K captures; close pushes will reveal the 1672x941 source limit.

Downloaded binary: [Real-ESRGAN v0.2.5.0 macOS release](https://github.com/xinntao/Real-ESRGAN/releases/tag/v0.2.5.0), archive realesrgan-ncnn-vulkan-20220424-macos.zip. Command: realesrgan-ncnn-vulkan -i input.png -o output.png -s 4 -n realesrgan-x4plus -m /tmp/daybreak-realesrgan/models -t 128.

## Cutouts and checks

The six requested cutouts are 3840x2160 RGBA: p01_storm, p02_dark, p04_wide, p06_unfold, p11_dawn and p12_day. A local extraction adapted the supplied navy-background method. It uses float RGB distances, connected exterior background removal, a narrow feather and neighbouring opaque colour at the edge to suppress navy halos. Large enclosed navy gaps between wires are removed; small dark details are retained. Orange-background evidence is in qa/*_on_orange.jpg and qa/cutout_orange_contact.jpg.

Raw, enlarged, transparent and comparison files remain available for review. Contact sheet uses the final enlarged plates in film order. A reference match means the recurring handmade village materials and layout were visually checked; these are generated photographs, so tiny construction details are not pixel-identical across cameras.

## Attempt outcomes and remaining compromises

### p01_storm

- 1: selected. Matches the reference village; physical cotton cloud and rain, warm windows, calm navy left. Fine rain strands and suspension wires remain delicate.

### p02_dark

- 1: rejected for too much lower-left terrain entering the headline column.
- 2: selected. Two workers, flashlight, broken concrete pole and mixed lit/dark houses. Lower-left vegetation still reaches about 35% of image width; upper-left headline region is clear.

### p03_meter

- 1: rejected for lower-left ground entering the negative space and meter reading as a plain enclosure.
- 2: selected. Red horseshoe magnet and unmarked grey analog meter enclosure on weathered plaster, warm practical light; approximately 40% calm navy at left.

### p03_solar

- 1: rejected because the house and foliage extended too far left.
- 2: selected. Harsh physical midday light, dark blue rooftop panels; approximately 40% navy left, slight leaf intrusion near the lower boundary.

### p04_wide

- 1: rejected: twelve trucks instead of eleven.
- 2: rejected: still twelve trucks despite the count correction.
- 3: selected. Exactly eleven separate white bucket trucks counted in the final crop. Same depot and village, calm left. Empty gravel gap at the left of the row is intentional.

### p06_unfold

- 1: rejected: flat side too oblique and construction too far left.
- 2: rejected: negative space still too narrow.
- 3: selected as best. Tactile miniature village across a folded physical surface, curled hemisphere at right. Remaining compromise: flat half is seen from a steep overhead angle rather than strictly orthographic; left calm width is about 37.5%, not a full 40%.

### p07_map

- 1: rejected: oblique camera; front facades prominent.
- 2: rejected: closer to top-down but visible front facades remain.
- 3: selected. Vertical overhead roof and truck-roof view, continuous landscape edge to edge. The reference's fallen pole remains on the right branch; account for it when drawing routes.

### p10_house

- 1: rejected: grille emblem and meter readout detail.
- 2: selected. Rear/side view of unbranded truck, plain meter enclosure with ambiguous dark object on top. House left of centre. Right upper region has over 40% dark room; curved ground reduces clear width near the lower edge to about 35%.

### p11_dawn

- 1: rejected: too much front light and tree canopy entering the upper half.
- 2: selected. Dark village and trucks with warm edge light from the hidden right-hand source, no visible sun disc. Upper half essentially plain navy; tallest fine leaves slightly cross the midpoint.

### p12_day

- 1: selected. Same village, three trucks, orange/cyan/electric-blue physical satin ribbons. Planet left edge is about 37%, close to but slightly inside the requested 40% headline allowance.
