# Daybreak keyframes v2: un-slopped plates (generated with Codex image)

Date: 2026-11-07, Bucharest. Requested by Teo (team lead).

## What went wrong in v1 (look at it: /tmp/cp-rejected-v1-codex.jpg)

Teo: "absolutely horrible, looks exactly vibe-coded and slop, you can tell it is slop
from a distance, those are the generic AI colours." He is right. v1 hit every AI
default at once: violet/magenta/pink gradients, neon glowing lines, bloom and halos,
a sunset-gradient sky, gradient-filled numbers, frosted glass cards, 3D clay
"emoji" objects on app-icon tiles, a floating isometric map, fake UI, and the same
ultra-heavy geometric font baked into every image.

Also rejected earlier, for the opposite reason (too empty and grey):
/tmp/cp-rejected-v3.png and /tmp/cp-rejected-brandlab.png.

## The fix: real photography from the subject's world, type set later in code

1. **No text in the images at all.** You produce text-free plates. The headlines are
   set afterwards in code with a real typeface, so they are exact and do not carry
   the AI-font tell. Leave the negative space each spec asks for.
2. **Real photography, made the way a top commercial photographer would make it.**
   A real camera and lens (named in each spec), natural or studio light that makes
   physical sense, believable imperfection: dust, wear, mud, scuffs, rain. It must
   pass as a real photo in a high-end campaign, not as a render.
3. **Colour comes from real things in the world of Romanian power distribution**,
   never from an AI palette: storm slate and wheat gold of the Banat plain, concrete
   poles with ceramic insulators, white bucket trucks, fluorescent hi-vis orange and
   yellow workwear, retroreflective silver tape, coloured hard hats. Colour is
   allowed to be bold and even a whole rainbow, but every colour must be the colour
   of a real object or a real light.
4. **Apple-level restraint.** One subject, a lot of clean space, precise light. The
   craft of Apple product photography and Apple "Shot on iPhone" billboards.

## Hard bans (if any of these appear, regenerate)

purple, violet, magenta or pink as a mood colour; any gradient sky that looks like a
wallpaper; neon; glow, bloom, halo, light leaks, lens flare, god rays; glass or
frosted panels; 3D render look, clay, plastic toy look, emoji style, app-icon tiles,
rounded squares; isometric floating tiles; HDR, crushed teal-and-orange grade, over-
sharpening, over-saturation; particles, sparks, magic dust; lightning bolts; perfect
symmetry with a halo behind the object; stock-photo smiles; text, letters, digits,
logos, brand marks, watermarks; recognisable faces.

## Two directions, four plates each

Two agents work in parallel, one per direction. Same four story beats in both, so
Teo can compare the directions side by side:
1. hook (storm), 2. "where do crews go first?", 3. the three problems, 4. the name.

Landscape 16:9, the largest size the tool gives. Plates go in
`presentation/frames-codex/v2/plates/`.
