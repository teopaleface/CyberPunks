# Plate specs (text-free)

## Direction 1: "First light" (documentary photography, Apple "Shot on iPhone" billboard grammar)

Editorial documentary photography of the Banat plain in western Romania. Shot on a
full-frame camera, natural light only, colour like Kodak Portra 400 film: soft
contrast, true colours, fine grain, nothing pushed. Colours come from the land and
the light: storm slate, wheat gold, sunflower yellow, dry clay, pale dawn sky. Romanian
details: concrete distribution poles with ceramic insulators, low village houses with
terracotta roofs, white utility bucket trucks without logos.

- **d1_storm.png** — 24mm, low horizon at the bottom 25%. A towering grey-green shelf
  cloud rolls over a flat field of sunflowers bending in the wind; a line of concrete
  poles with sagging wires runs to the horizon. Real dusk light, a strip of warm light
  under the cloud edge. NO lightning. The upper-middle sky (a calm, dark cloud mass) is
  empty, for a white headline.
- **d1_where.png** — 35mm, eye level. A rural road at first light, five white utility
  bucket trucks parked in a line on the gravel shoulder, headlights on, cold blue
  morning with the first warm sun touching the truck tops; one lineman in a
  fluorescent orange hi-vis jacket and white hard hat seen from behind, walking to the
  first truck. Trucks and person sit in the lower 45% of the frame; the sky above is
  clean, for a headline.
- **d1_problems.png** — a triptych: three photos side by side, equal width, thin white
  gutters, each one documentary and real: (1) a concrete pole snapped by a storm,
  lying across a muddy field with tangled wires; (2) an old electricity meter in an
  opened grey metal box on a village wall, a strong round magnet stuck on the meter's
  front (meter tampering), shot with a little flash; (3) dark blue rooftop solar panels
  on a terracotta village roof under a harsh, white midday sun. Keep the bottom 20% of
  each photo calm and plain (sky, wall or roof), for a label.
- **d1_dawn.png** — 50mm, horizon at the bottom 30%. The sun just breaking a perfectly
  flat horizon over a harvested wheat field, one line of concrete poles with wires in
  silhouette; the sky is natural and pale (soft blue above, warm peach only near the
  horizon), not a saturated gradient. A huge, empty, calm sky for a wordmark.

## Direction 2: "Hi-vis" (bold colour from safety workwear, studio product photography)

Studio product photography of real utility field gear on seamless paper backdrops
in bold safety colours, lit like Apple product photography: one large soft key light,
a gentle real floor shadow where the object touches the seamless sweep, crisp
detail, real wear on the objects (scuffs, mud, dust). Each plate is drenched in one
real safety colour that fills the whole frame: fluorescent orange like a hi-vis
jacket (#FF5B1A), hi-vis yellow-green like a safety vest (#DDF21B), signal cobalt
blue like a work glove (#1E4FD8), and retroreflective silver. Flat, solid, matte
backdrop colour. No gradients on the backdrop, no glow on anything.

- **d2_storm.png** — fluorescent orange seamless. A white utility hard hat, scuffed,
  covered in heavy real raindrops, water pooling on the floor around it, placed in the
  lower-centre third, slightly turned. The top 45% of the frame is plain orange, for a
  headline.
- **d2_where.png** — signal cobalt blue seamless. Five real hard hats in a tidy row on
  the floor, in five real helmet colours: white, yellow, orange, red, green. Seen
  slightly from above, evenly spaced, centred in the lower half. Top half plain blue,
  for a headline.
- **d2_problems.png** — a triptych of three equal panels side by side, no gutters, each
  its own solid seamless colour: (1) hi-vis yellow-green: the top of a broken concrete
  pole fragment with a ceramic insulator and a snapped cable; (2) signal cobalt blue: a
  real residential electricity meter with a round magnet stuck on its front; (3)
  fluorescent orange: one solar panel at an angle catching a hard studio light. One
  object per panel, centred in the upper 70%; the bottom 25% of each panel is plain
  colour, for a label.
- **d2_dawn.png** — a macro photograph of a fluorescent yellow-green hi-vis vest fabric
  filling the frame, with one retroreflective silver stripe running across the lower
  third and catching the light the way real reflective tape does under a camera flash
  (bright but not glowing beyond its edges). The upper 60% is plain fabric, for a
  wordmark.
