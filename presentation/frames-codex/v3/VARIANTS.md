# Hi-vis, three colour variants (text-free plates)

Teo picked direction 2 "Hi-vis" from v2 (see ../v2/contact_sheet.jpg, right column):
"I like the right one a lot, but maybe it is too colourful." So we keep everything
that works (real studio product photography of used field gear, seamless paper
backdrops, Apple-level light and restraint, no text) and vary only HOW MUCH colour.

All hard bans from ../v2/BRIEF.md still apply. Read it.

Shared photographic language (identical in all three variants): studio product
photography of real, used utility field gear on seamless paper, lit like Apple
product photography: one large soft key light from above-left, a gentle real floor
shadow where the object meets the seamless sweep, crisp detail, real wear (scuffs,
mud, dust, scratches). 85mm lens look, no distortion. Backdrops are flat, matte,
solid: no gradients, no vignettes, no glow. Objects must have no text, logos or
digits (meters have a blank display). Landscape 16:9, largest size available.

Same four beats in every variant: storm, where, problems, dawn (the name frame).
Leave the negative space described, the headline is set later in code.

Note for "storm" in all variants: raindrops ON the helmet only. No puddle on the
floor (in v2 the puddle reflected the orange backdrop and read as blood).

## h1 "One colour" (least colour: safety orange is the only colour in the whole film)

Neutral backdrops (warm concrete grey #CFCBC4, off-white #EEEBE5), black, and one
accent: fluorescent safety orange #FF5B1A, which appears in every frame, either as
one object or, once, as the whole frame.
- h1_storm.png — concrete grey seamless. One fluorescent orange hard hat, scuffed,
  beaded with heavy raindrops, lower-centre third, slightly turned. Top 45% plain grey.
- h1_where.png — off-white seamless. Five hard hats in a tidy row, lower half: four
  white, and the first one on the left fluorescent orange. Top half plain.
- h1_problems.png — triptych of three equal panels, no gutters, all on the same
  concrete grey seamless: (1) the top of a broken concrete pole with a ceramic
  insulator and a snapped cable; (2) a residential electricity meter with a round
  magnet stuck on its front; (3) one solar panel at an angle catching hard light.
  Object centred in the upper 70% of each panel; bottom 25% plain grey.
- h1_dawn.png — macro of fluorescent orange hi-vis vest fabric filling the whole
  frame, one retroreflective silver stripe across the lower third. Upper 60% plain.

## h2 "Dark to light" (colour tells the story: night at the start, daybreak at the end)

Low-key studio on a deep graphite seamless (#2B2C2E) for the problem beats; the only
colour comes from the objects themselves, lit by one hard key light with a soft fill.
The film brightens beat by beat and ends drenched in hi-vis yellow-green.
- h2_storm.png — graphite seamless, low-key. A white hard hat beaded with raindrops,
  lit by one hard cold-white light from the left, deep shadows. Top 45% dark plain.
- h2_where.png — graphite seamless. Five hard hats in a row in real helmet colours
  (white, yellow, orange, red, green), lit from the front so the colours glow on the
  dark ground the way real plastic does under a spotlight (no added glow). Top half
  plain dark.
- h2_problems.png — triptych, no gutters, all graphite: the broken pole fragment with
  insulator, the meter with a magnet, the solar panel; each with a crisp rim light.
  Bottom 25% of each panel plain dark.
- h2_dawn.png — macro of fluorescent yellow-green hi-vis vest fabric filling the frame,
  retroreflective stripe across the lower third. Upper 60% plain. (Bright finale.)

## h3 "Two colours" (orange and cobalt only, alternating; no yellow, no rainbow)

Two drenched backdrop colours, fluorescent safety orange #FF5B1A and signal cobalt
#1E4FD8, plus white and black. The objects are neutral (white, grey, silver,
concrete, black), so the backdrop is the only colour.
- h3_storm.png — cobalt seamless. A white hard hat beaded with raindrops, lower-centre.
  Top 45% plain cobalt.
- h3_where.png — orange seamless. Five white hard hats in a tidy row, lower half.
  Top half plain orange.
- h3_problems.png — triptych, no gutters: panels orange / cobalt / orange, with the
  broken pole fragment and insulator, the meter with a magnet, the solar panel.
  Bottom 25% of each panel plain colour.
- h3_dawn.png — macro of a cobalt work-jacket fabric filling the frame with one
  retroreflective silver stripe across the lower third. Upper 60% plain cobalt.
