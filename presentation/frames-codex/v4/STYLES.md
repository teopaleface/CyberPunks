# Daybreak, three more styles (text-free plates)

Teo's feedback on v2/v3: he likes the "Hi-vis" kind of frame (one subject, solid
colour, big headline, easy to animate), but real-life photography "feels too sad".
He wants other styles of that kind: upbeat, clean, Apple-level, and built so the
elements can be animated later (subject clearly separated from a plain background,
few large shapes, layers that could move on their own).

All hard bans from ../v2/BRIEF.md still apply (no purple/pink mood colour, neon,
glow, flare, glass, 3D-render/clay/emoji look, gradient wallpaper skies, text,
logos, digits). Read it. Also look at ../v2/contact_sheet.jpg (right column = the
liked look) and /tmp/cp-rejected-v1-codex.jpg (AI slop, never).

Same four beats in every style, same negative space rule: leave the area named in
each beat empty and plain, the headline is set later in code.
1. **storm** — "Storm hits Banat." Leave the top-left 45% plain.
2. **where** — "Where do crews go first?" Leave the top half plain.
3. **problems** — triptych of three equal panels: fallen pole / electricity meter with a
   magnet stuck on it (stolen power) / solar panel under a blazing sun (too much sun).
   Leave the bottom 25% of each panel plain for a label.
4. **dawn** — the name frame "Daybreak". Leave the upper 55% plain.

Landscape 16:9, largest size available.

## s1 "Miniature world"

A real, handmade scale-model diorama (1:87 model-railway scale), the kind a master
model maker builds: tiny white utility bucket trucks, tiny workers in orange hi-vis,
concrete poles with thread-thin wires, a small Banat village with terracotta roofs,
sunflower fields made of flock. Photographed with a real macro lens, shallow depth of
field from the lens (not fake blur), bright, cheerful studio daylight. Real model
paint colours, warm and happy but never neon. Each diorama sits on a clean plain
seamless backdrop (pale sky blue #BFD8EE or warm off-white #F1EEE8) so it can be cut
out and animated. Joyful, precise, a little witty.
- s1_storm: a small round diorama of village and poles; above it a real cotton-wool
  storm cloud hangs on a thin wire (the model maker's trick), two poles tilted, tiny
  resin puddles, tiny workers looking up. Backdrop pale blue-grey.
- s1_where: five tiny white bucket trucks lined up in a model depot yard, tiny workers
  in orange walking to them, seen from 45 degrees above.
- s1_problems: three small round dioramas on wooden bases, one per panel: a fallen pole
  with tiny workers; a village house wall with a tiny meter and a tiny magnet on it;
  a roof with solar panels under a strong little lamp "sun".
- s1_dawn: the whole little village at warm low morning light, tiny trucks driving out
  on the roads with long shadows; the upper part is plain warm backdrop.

## s2 "Pictogram poster"

Flat graphic design in the International Typographic Style, with pictograms in the
tradition of 1970s Olympic pictograms and ISO safety signs: bold geometric shapes,
one consistent stroke weight, rounded geometry, strict grid alignment, solid flat
colour fields, no gradients, no shadows, no textures, no 3D, no people illustrations
in the "corporate flat" style. Upbeat through confident colour: safety orange
#FF5B1A, signal yellow #FFC400, cobalt #1E4FD8, white, black; at most three colours
per frame. Every shape is flat and separate, perfect for animation.
- s2_storm: on cobalt, a big geometric cloud, three slanted rain bars, and a leaning
  pole pictogram with two wires; white and black shapes.
- s2_where: on signal yellow, five identical utility-truck pictograms in a row (black),
  the first one orange.
- s2_problems: three panels, each its own colour: a fallen-pole pictogram; a meter
  pictogram with a horseshoe magnet; a sun pictogram over a solar-panel pictogram.
- s2_dawn: on safety orange, a white half-circle sun rising behind a straight black
  horizon line with three small pole pictograms.

## s3 "Cut paper"

Handmade layered paper craft: real coloured card stock cut by hand, stacked in a few
layers with small real shadows between layers, photographed straight from the front
with soft, even light; paper fibre texture visible. Bold, simple shapes, few layers,
like the paper work of a top editorial illustrator, never fussy or fantasy-detailed.
Colours of real artist papers: tomato red, mustard, cobalt, mint, cream, black.
Every layer is a separate piece of paper, ideal for parallax animation.
- s3_storm: layered grey-blue paper clouds, paper rain strips, a paper pole leaning,
  striped paper fields below.
- s3_where: five paper bucket trucks in a row on a paper road, cream background.
- s3_problems: three panels, each its own paper colour: fallen pole; meter with a
  magnet; solar panel under a big paper sun.
- s3_dawn: a paper sun half-circle rising behind layered paper fields and three poles;
  upper part plain cream paper.
