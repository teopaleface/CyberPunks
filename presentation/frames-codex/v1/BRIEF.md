## The product, in plain words (so the frames tell a story anyone gets)

Daybreak is software for the night-shift dispatchers of Rețele Electrice, the
electricity distribution company of PPC in Romania. Every evening a dispatcher has
to decide where tomorrow's field crews go. The work comes from three kinds of
problems: storm damage (fallen poles), meters that are broken or tampered with
(stolen power), and too much rooftop solar pushing voltage too high. Daybreak looks
at all of it and builds the plan for every crew in one click. When it is not sure,
it asks the dispatcher to approve. In a 30-day simulation it delivered 49% more
value with the same crews.

## Style bible (identical for every frame)

- Landscape 16:9, the largest size the image tool gives.
- The look of an Apple keynote slide or an apple.com product page: premium, crisp,
  one idea per frame, a lot of empty space, centred composition, studio-quality
  light, flawless materials. It must look like a real product launch, not a
  template, not stock photography, not clip-art, no generic flat icons.
- Colour: the "Daybreak spectrum", a sunrise. Night indigo #0B1033, violet #6E4BFF,
  magenta-pink #FF4FA3, coral #FF6B4A, amber #FFB23F, sky cyan #4FC3FF, and mint
  #2ED3A0 only for "approve". Full colour is welcome, including a smooth luminous
  rainbow gradient (like a sunrise, or the glowing colour ring of an AI assistant).
  Each frame has ONE dominant colour mood so the film travels from night (violet,
  indigo) to sunrise (pink, coral, amber) to day (bright, warm). Never dull grey,
  never flat corporate blue, never muddy.
- Headline typography: very large, bold, clean sans-serif like a premium keynote
  (heavy weight, tight letter spacing), white on dark or near-black on light.
  The headline must be spelled EXACTLY as given, with the exact punctuation.
  At most one small subline when the frame spec gives one. No other text anywhere
  except the UI labels a frame spec lists explicitly.
- No logos or brand marks of any company (no Apple logo, no PPC logo, no sun-over-
  route logo), no watermarks, no recognisable faces, no text on vehicles or objects.

## Text check (mandatory)

After each generation, look at the image closely. If any word of the headline,
subline or UI labels is misspelled, has a missing or extra letter, wrong diacritic
or stray text, regenerate. Up to 3 attempts per frame; keep the best one and say
honestly in the log if a flaw remains.
