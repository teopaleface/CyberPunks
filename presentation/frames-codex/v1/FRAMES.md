# The 10 frames (story order)

The story: problem → who has it → the product → how it works → trust → result → who it is for.
Every frame must be understood in one second by someone who knows nothing about power grids.

## Part A (frames 1–5)

**f01_storm.png** — Headline: `Storm hits Banat.`
A towering storm cell at night over flat farmland, one vivid violet-magenta lightning
bolt, a thin line of wooden power poles with wires along the horizon. Cinematic,
saturated, clean, huge sky. Headline centred in the sky, white. Mood: violet night.

**f02_dark.png** — Headline: `Homes go dark.`
Aerial night view of a small town street grid. A clean diagonal boundary: one half
still glowing warm amber with house lights, the other half completely dark, deep
indigo. Striking and simple, nothing else. Headline white, centred over the dark half
or the top. Mood: indigo.

**f03_where.png** — Headline: `Where do crews go first?`
Top-down aerial of five white utility bucket trucks parked in a row in a depot at
blue hour, headlights on, long soft shadows, a faint pink-violet dawn tint on the
asphalt. No text or logos on the trucks. Headline white, large, top area. Mood:
blue hour turning violet.

**f04_problems.png** — Three short lines, exactly:
`Fallen poles.` / `Stolen power.` / `Too much sun.`
Three glossy premium 3D objects in a row, each on its own rounded vivid colour tile:
a snapped wooden power pole with a small spark (violet tile), a smart electricity
meter with a glowing magnet stuck to it and a small red warning light (cyan tile),
a rooftop solar panel under a blazing sun (amber tile). Soft studio light, like
premium product renders. Each line of text sits under its own tile, or the three
lines stack in large type above the tiles. Mood: three colours, balanced.

**f05_daybreak.png** — Wordmark: `Daybreak` — subline: `Tomorrow's crews, planned tonight.`
The pivot of the film. A dark night sky splits open into a luminous sunrise spectrum
(indigo → violet → pink → coral → amber) rising from the horizon behind the word.
The word `Daybreak` is huge, white, bold, centred. No logo or symbol, only the word.
Mood: sunrise.

## Part B (frames 6–10)

**f06_one_click.png** — Headline: `One click. Every crew planned.`
A premium product shot: a sleek silver laptop at a slight three-quarter angle on a
vivid gradient background (violet to pink), a soft rainbow glow around the screen
edge. The screen shows a clean, light, modern app: on the left a simple map with
three coloured routes (cyan, pink, amber), each with 3–4 numbered stops; on the
right three cards `Crew 1`, `Crew 2`, `Crew 3` with matching colour dots; top right
one dark rounded button `Plan tomorrow`; a large white macOS-style cursor clicking it.
App title in the top-left of the window: `Daybreak`. No other UI text. No brand logo
on the laptop. Headline above the laptop, white.

**f07_map.png** — Headline: `Every crew knows where to go.`
A beautiful minimal illustrated map: a soft matte 3D landscape tile (gentle terrain,
a river, faint roads) floating on deep indigo. Three luminous glowing routes in cyan,
pink and amber, each leaving one depot dot and passing 3–4 round stop pins. No labels,
no place names, no numbers. Clean, readable from far away. Headline white, top.
Mood: indigo with neon colour.

**f08_asks.png** — Headline: `Not sure? It asks you.`
One large frosted-glass card floating on a soft vivid pink-to-violet gradient. On the
card: a small amber dot with the label `Not sure`, the question `Check this meter?`,
and two buttons: a mint green filled `Approve` and a quiet outlined `Skip`. A large
white cursor about to click `Approve`. Nothing else on the card. Headline white above.

**f09_result.png** — Big number: `+49%` — line below: `more value. Same crews.` —
tiny footnote: `30-day simulation`
Pure black background. The number `+49%` is gigantic, filled with the full luminous
sunrise spectrum gradient (violet → pink → coral → amber), like a keynote stat slide.
The line under it in white, the footnote small and grey. Nothing else.

**f10_end.png** — Wordmark: `Daybreak` — subline: `For Rețele Electrice dispatchers` —
small line: `CyberPunks · SmartHack 2026`
Deep night-indigo background with a thin, glowing sunrise-spectrum arc rising at the
bottom like a horizon at first light (an elegant event-invitation look). `Daybreak`
huge, white, centred. Subline in white below, small line in grey at the bottom. No
logo or symbol. Note the Romanian letter ț in `Rețele` (t with comma below).
