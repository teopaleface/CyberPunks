# Daybreak v6: push s1 "Miniature world" and g2 "White model" to maximum wow

Teo picked two styles from ../styles_overview.jpg: s1 "Miniature world" and g2 "White
architectural model". His words: "make them better, as wow and as strong as possible".
All hard bans of ../v2/BRIEF.md still apply (no purple/pink mood colour, neon, glow,
flare, glass, CGI/clay/toy-render look, gradient wallpaper skies, text, logos, digits).

What "wow" means here:
- **Craft you can feel.** The level of a museum-grade model, photographed by the best
  commercial product photographer. Every tiny detail is believable and intentional.
- **One continuous world.** Every frame shows THE SAME place (the same village, the
  same depot, the same five trucks), so the film feels like one camera moving through
  one world, and the frames can be animated together.
- **Light that tells the story.** Storm = cool, overcast. Problems = neutral studio.
  Map and name = warm, low morning sun with long shadows (daybreak).
- **Wit and life** (s1 especially): small human details that reward a second look.
- **Clean, Apple-level composition.** One clear subject, lots of plain space for the
  headline (set later in code), crisp focus on what matters.
- **Story you get in one second**, even with the sound off.

Landscape 16:9, largest size available. Five beats per style (headline in brackets
is only for your composition; never render text):
1. storm ("Storm hits Banat.") — leave the top-left 45% plain.
2. where ("Where do crews go first?") — leave the top 45% plain.
3. problems ("Fallen poles. / Stolen power. / Too much sun.") — three vignettes side by
   side, bottom 22% plain for labels.
4. map ("Every crew knows where to go.") — leave the top 35% plain.
5. dawn (the name "Daybreak") — leave the top 50% plain.

## s1 "Miniature world" — world bible

A handmade 1:87 diorama by a master model maker, of a small village on the Banat plain
in western Romania: whitewashed houses with terracotta tile roofs, wooden fences, a
sunflower field, a dirt road and an asphalt road, grey concrete utility poles with
fine dark wires, walnut trees, and a small utility depot with five white bucket trucks
(a thin orange stripe on each, no text). Tiny workers in fluorescent orange hi-vis and
white hard hats. Witty life details: a cat on a roof, laundry on a line, a worker with
a thermos, a dog. The diorama sits on a round walnut-wood plinth with a clean bevelled
edge, on a plain seamless backdrop. Photographed with a real macro lens: shallow depth
of field from the lens (front and back softly out of focus, the subject razor sharp),
camera close to model eye level for drama unless the beat says otherwise. Real model
paint colours, warm and happy, never neon. Tiny practical lights where they make
sense (warm window lights, truck headlights).

- **s1_storm** — pale blue-grey seamless. Camera low at village eye level. Above the
  village hangs a big storm cloud of real grey cotton wool on a thin wire; rain made of
  many clear fishing-line strands with tiny drops; one concrete pole snapped and lying
  across the road, wires down; tiny workers in orange looking up, one holding a
  flashlight. Cool overcast light. Village in the lower right; top-left plain.
- **s1_where** — warm off-white seamless. The depot: five white bucket trucks parked
  at slight angles (not a rigid row), headlights on, crews loading tools, one worker
  checking a clipboard, a coffee thermos on a bumper; early blue-hour light with warm
  headlights. Seen from about 30 degrees above. Depot in the lower 55%.
- **s1_problems** — warm off-white seamless. Three small round walnut plinths side by
  side, one story each: (1) a snapped concrete pole with wires down and two tiny workers
  in orange; (2) a village house wall with a meter box, a large red horseshoe magnet
  stuck on the meter and a tiny suspicious figure in a dark hoodie tiptoeing away;
  (3) a house roof covered in solar panels under a tiny brass sun lamp on a thin rod,
  the air shimmering with heat (a tiny worker wiping his brow). Plinths in the upper 75%.
- **s1_map** — warm cream seamless. Top-down view, 70 degrees from above, of the whole
  village diorama. From the depot, three colour-coded ribbons of thin satin tape run
  along the roads, each to three or four houses marked with tiny round map pins: one
  ribbon fluorescent orange, one cobalt blue, one green. A truck at the head of each
  ribbon. Warm low morning sun from the right, long shadows. The ribbons are bold and
  easy to read from far away. Diorama in the lower 65%.
- **s1_dawn** — warm pale peach seamless. The whole village on its round plinth at
  sunrise: a small real warm lamp low on the right acts as the rising sun, long shadows,
  window lights still on, the five trucks driving out along the road in a line with
  headlights on. Camera at a low three-quarter angle. Diorama in the lower 50%.

## g2 "White architectural model" — world bible

A competition-grade architectural model, as built by the best architecture studios:
matte white card, white basswood and white 3D-printed resin, razor-crisp laser-cut
edges, perfect joints, tiny precise details (window recesses, roof tiles as fine
grooves, fences as hair-thin strips, white trees as faceted resin forms). The model
shows the same Banat village and depot as above, entirely in white. The ONLY colour in
the whole film comes from the things that matter: the crew trucks in fluorescent orange,
the route threads (orange, cobalt, green), and a few tiny accents named per beat. Shot
like an Apple product photograph: big soft key light, deep clean shadows, crisp focus,
shallow depth of field, plain pale warm-grey table and backdrop. Calm, precise, luxurious.

- **g2_storm** — cool overcast light. Low camera. A large storm cloud of grey felt hangs
  on an invisible wire over the white village; rain as fine clear acrylic rods; one white
  pole snapped across a white road; a single fluorescent orange truck stopped beside it.
  Village in the lower right; top-left plain.
- **g2_where** — the white depot yard, camera closer and lower than before (35 degrees),
  five fluorescent orange bucket trucks parked at slight angles, crisp and large in frame,
  long soft shadows from a low light. Depot in the lower 55%.
- **g2_problems** — three white square plinths side by side, no lamps or rigs visible:
  (1) a white pole snapped with hair-thin wires down; (2) a white house wall with a meter
  box and a fluorescent orange horseshoe magnet on it; (3) a white roof with dark blue
  solar panels and a small polished brass sphere on a thin rod above it as the sun.
  Plinths in the upper 75%.
- **g2_map** — the whole white village from 60 degrees above, low warm morning light
  from the right, long shadows. From the depot, three bold coloured threads run along
  the white streets to three or four houses each, held by round map pins with coloured
  heads: orange, cobalt, green. The threads are thick enough to read instantly. Model in
  the lower 65%.
- **g2_dawn** — low warm sunrise light raking across the white village from the right,
  long golden shadows on white, five orange trucks driving out in a line along the main
  road. Low three-quarter camera. Model in the lower 50%.
